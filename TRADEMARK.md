# N-Toki trademark policy

The code is MIT so the toki standard can spread anywhere. The name is what tells people which one is the original. This policy keeps both true.

"N-Toki", the N-Toki wordmark, the seal-ring logo, and the companion illustrations are trademarks of the N-Toki project (intimate-avocado). They are not covered by the MIT or CC BY-SA licenses.

## You may, without asking

- Use the engine, the spec and the app under their licenses, in anything.
- Say that your product is "built on N-Toki", "uses the N-Toki engine", or "compatible with N-Toki".
- Link to, screenshot, review, teach, or write about N-Toki by name.
- Fork the repo and keep the name while your fork is a faithful, unmodified or lightly modified copy clearly linked to this repository.

## Please ask first

- Naming a product, app, service, company or domain "N-Toki", "NToki", "N Toki" or anything confusingly close to it.
- Using the logo or the companion art in your own branding.
- Charging for anything that carries the N-Toki name.

## Not allowed

- Presenting a modified version as the official N-Toki, or implying endorsement.
- Using the name on anything that breaks VALUES.md: accounts required, tracking, ads, streaks, premium tiers, or a "you're late" mechanic. That is the one thing the name exists to guarantee.

Rename your fork and everything above is yours to do. Questions: open an issue.

*This is a community policy, not legal advice; a registered mark may follow.*
