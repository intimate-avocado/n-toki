## 时间 · Time

### 他们拿走了 · They took it

Once, noon was when the sun was highest — where *you* were. Every town had its own noon. Then came the railways and the telegraph, and on 18 November 1883 noon was voted into zones so the trains could keep a schedule.

That was the first taking. Time stopped belonging to the sky and started belonging to the timetable.

### 他们卖了回来 · They sold it back

Then came the whistle, the punch clock, the timesheet. Time became something you sell by the hour. Then it became something you *manage* — planners, apps, systems, courses.

The trick is old: take something, then sell it back. The more you optimize the day, the less of it you have. The more you track the hours, the faster they go. You are not behind. You were put behind.

### 兔子 · The Rabbit

The White Rabbit is late. He checks his watch. He runs. Alice follows him down and everything gets strange.

But there is another rabbit. 卯 — sunrise, spring, east, the hour that *begins* the day. Not the hour you are behind on. The one that opens.

Same animal. Different story. One you chase. One that marks where you already are.

### 环 · The Loop

Time is not a line with a start and an end. You cannot lose your place on a line that never had one.

It is a loop. An instance. A spiral that comes back around and is never quite the same. A wave that collapses into *now* — and *now* is the only place time is ever actually measured.

Yesterday was a dream. Tomorrow never came. Only this, and this, and this.

### 玻璃盒 · The Glass Box

You can try to trap a moment. Build a box, freeze it, keep it. But what you trap is a photograph of the moment, and the moment is already gone.

Some moments don't need a box. They stay on their own. You don't have to hold them — they hold you.

### 这里不是什么 · What this is not

No account. No tracking. No notifications telling you that you are late. No streaks. No premium tier that unlocks *more time*.

Nothing here is measured against a deadline, because there is no deadline. There is a sun, and there is you, and there is now.

### 为什么免费 · Why it is free

N-Toki is free and open source because a timekeeper should be auditable, and because the alternative is selling you back the day you already earned.
